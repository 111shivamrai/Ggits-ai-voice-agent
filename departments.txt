DEPARTMENTS AND FACULTY INFORMATION — GGITS (Updated from Official Website)
=============================================================================

GGITS has significantly expanded its departments compared to older data.
It now offers 12+ B.Tech specializations across multiple departments.

DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING (CSE)
------------------------------------------------------
Status: NBA ACCREDITED
Code: CS
- Largest and most popular department
- Offers multiple specializations under the CS umbrella:
  → Pure CSE
  → AIML (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)
  → Data Science
  → CSBS (Computer Science and Business Systems) — TCS co-designed
  → IoT+Cybersecurity+Blockchain
  → CSD (Computer Science and Design)
  → Robotics and Artificial Intelligence
- Labs: Programming Lab, Data Structures Lab, AI/ML Lab,
  Networking Lab, Cyber Security Lab, Cloud Computing Lab,
  Web Development Lab, IoT Lab, Robotics Lab
- AWS Tech Alliance Partner — students get AWS cloud access
- IBM and Microsoft department collaborations
- Oracle Academy programs
- Coding Club active
- Students participate in Smart India Hackathon, FossHack

DEPARTMENT OF CSE — AIML (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Code: AL
- One of the most in-demand branches in India right now
- Curriculum: Python, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Neural Networks,
  Computer Vision, NLP, Reinforcement Learning, AI Ethics
- Real-world project building from first year itself
- EXAMPLE: First-year student Shivam Rai built a live AI Voice Agent
  using Vapi.ai, Node.js, React, MongoDB — deployed on Vercel and Render
- Career: AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, NLP Engineer,
  AI Researcher, Computer Vision Engineer

DEPARTMENT OF CSE — DATA SCIENCE
----------------------------------
Code: CD
- Curriculum: Statistics, Python, R, Big Data, Data Visualization,
  Business Intelligence, Data Engineering, SQL, NoSQL
- Industry tools: Tableau, Power BI, Spark, Hadoop
- Career: Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Data Engineer

DEPARTMENT OF CSE — CSBS (Computer Science and Business Systems)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Code: CB
- Co-designed with TCS (Tata Consultancy Services)
- Unique: Combines computer science with business and management
- Curriculum: Programming + ERP + Business Analytics + Management
- TCS directly recruits from this program
- Career: IT Consultant, Business Analyst, Product Manager, ERP Developer

DEPARTMENT OF CSE — IoT, CYBER SECURITY AND BLOCKCHAIN
-------------------------------------------------------
Code: IS
