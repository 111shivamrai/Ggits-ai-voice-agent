INTERESTING QUESTIONS THE AI AGENT CAN ANSWER IMPRESSIVELY
------------------------------------------------------------
Q: "What is GGITS' highest salary package?"
A: Our highest package is 65 LPA — that was in 2023. In 2022 it was 56 LPA.
   Companies like Texas Instruments, product companies, and international firms
   have placed our students at these packages.

Q: "Is 96% placement rate real or just on paper?"
A: It's official and verified data published on our website with year-wise
   breakdown. You can see 418 offers from 44 companies in 2025 alone.
   Names like TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Tech Mahindra — all official and documented.

Q: "What does GGITS do for students who don't get placed?"
A: GGITS runs year-round training through the T&P cell.
   Students who need extra support get additional interview coaching,
   aptitude training, and are connected with companies in Jabalpur itself.

Q: "Has any GGITS student won any national competition?"
A: Yes! A CSE student won $3000 (₹2.3 lakh) in the Alibaba Cloud global contest.
   Multiple students have won in Oracle Academy drives.
   GGITS hosts IEEE international conferences — showing our research quality.
