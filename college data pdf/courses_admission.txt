ADMISSION PROCESS
------------------

Step 1: Eligibility Check
- For B.Tech: Must have passed 10+2 (Class 12) with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Minimum aggregate: 45% marks (40% for SC/ST candidates)
- Must have a valid JEE Main score (mandatory for RGPV-affiliated admissions)
- OR valid MP state DTE counselling rank

Step 2: Apply
- Register on the MP DTE portal (dte.mponline.gov.in)
- Fill the application form with personal, academic, and category details
- Upload required documents
- Pay the application fee online

Step 3: Counselling
- MP DTE conducts centralized online counselling
- Seat allotment based on JEE Main rank and choices filled
- Multiple rounds of counselling: Round 1, Round 2, Mop-Up Round

Step 4: Document Verification and Admission
- Report to the college on allotment letter date
- Submit original documents for verification
- Pay the first semester fee to confirm admission
