ADMISSION PROCESS
------------------
Step 1: Eligibility Check
- For B.Tech: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Minimum aggregate: 45% marks (40% for SC/ST candidates)
- Valid JEE Main score mandatory (for RGPV-affiliated admission)
- OR valid MP state DTE counselling rank

Step 2: Apply
- Register on MP DTE portal (dte.mponline.gov.in)
- Fill application with personal, academic, and category details
- Upload required documents and pay application fee online

Step 3: Counselling
- MP DTE conducts centralized online counselling
- Seat allotment based on JEE Main rank and choices filled
- Multiple rounds: Round 1, Round 2, Mop-Up Round

Step 4: Document Verification and Admission
- Report to college on allotment letter date
- Submit original documents
- Pay first semester fee to confirm admission

IMPORTANT ADMISSION DATES (Typical Year)

INTERESTING POINTS FOR AI AGENT
---------------------------------
Q: "GGITS offers AIML branch — how different is it from CSE?"
A: AIML is a specialized branch focused entirely on Artificial Intelligence,
   Machine Learning, Neural Networks, and Deep Learning. CSE is broader.
   AIML students go into AI companies, research labs, and product companies.
   Starting salaries for AIML are higher than traditional CSE in most cases.

Q: "Is CSBS a good branch?"
A: CSBS (Computer Science and Business Systems) is designed in collaboration
   with TCS — yes, TCS itself designed the curriculum! Students get both
   technical and business skills. Very useful for IT consulting and management roles.

Q: "What is the IoT+Cybersecurity branch?"
A: It is one of the newest, most in-demand branches. It covers Internet of Things
   device development AND cyber security AND blockchain — three technologies in one.
   Companies like Cisco, Palo Alto, and IBM hire from this specialization.
