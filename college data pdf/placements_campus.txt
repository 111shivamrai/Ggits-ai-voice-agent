PLACEMENTS AND CAMPUS LIFE
============================

PLACEMENT RECORD
-----------------
Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology and Science has a strong placement track record. The Training and Placement Cell works actively throughout the year to bring reputed companies to campus for recruitment.

- Average Placement Rate: 70% to 80% of eligible students placed annually
- Highest Package: Up to 10-12 LPA (for top performers in CSE/IT)
- Average Package: 3.5 to 5 LPA
- Companies Visit: 50+ companies visit campus every year

TRAINING AND PLACEMENT CELL
-----------------------------
- Dedicated Training and Placement Officer
- Pre-placement training provided to all final year students
- Training covers: Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, Technical Skills, Group Discussion, Personal Interview
- Mock interview sessions with industry experts
- Resume writing workshops
- Soft skills and communication training throughout the course
- Online coding practice on platforms like HackerRank and LeetCode (encouraged)

INTERNSHIP OPPORTUNITIES
--------------------------
- College facilitates summer internships for students after 2nd and 3rd year
- Students have interned at companies like Wipro, Infosys, BHEL, and local Jabalpur industries
- Internship certificate adds value to final placement
- Some internships convert into full-time job offers

ENTREPRENEURSHIP CELL
-----------------------
- Encourages students to build startups
- Mentorship provided by alumni entrepreneurs
- College supports participation in Smart India Hackathon and other national competitions

CAMPUS LIFE
------------
The campus of GGITS is vibrant and student-friendly. College believes in all-round development through academics, sports, cultural activities, and technical events.

ANNUAL EVENTS AND FESTS
------------------------
- Technomania: Annual technical festival with coding competitions, robotics, paper presentations, and technical quizzes
- Cultural Fest: Annual cultural event with music, dance, drama, fashion show, and other creative activities
- Sports Meet: Annual sports competition with cricket, football, volleyball, basketball, athletics
- Convocation Ceremony: Annual event for graduating students

STUDENT CLUBS AND ACTIVITIES
------------------------------
- Coding Club: Weekly contests, hackathons, and competitive programming sessions
- Robotics Club: Builds robots for competitions like Robocon
- Photography Club: Campus photography events and exhibitions
- Literary Club: Debates, essay competitions, creative writing
- NSS (National Service Scheme): Community service activities
- NCC (National Cadet Corps): Available for interested students

HOSTEL FACILITIES
------------------
Boys Hostel:
- Capacity: 200+ students
- Separate blocks with rooms for 2-3 students each
- Common room with TV
- 24x7 security
- Warden available on campus
- Mess with home-style food
- Wi-Fi connectivity
- Laundry facility nearby
- Gym/recreation area

Girls Hostel:
- Capacity: 150+ students
- Safe and secure campus with ladies warden
- Separate hostel premises with strict entry/exit policy
- All amenities similar to boys hostel
- 24x7 CCTV surveillance

HOSTEL FEE: Approximately Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 60,000 per year (including mess)

FOOD AND CAFETERIA
-------------------
- College canteen serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner
