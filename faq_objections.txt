FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================

Q: What is GGITS and where is it located?
A: Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology and Sciences, known as GGITS,
   is one of the top engineering colleges in Central India.
   Located at Tilwara Ghat Road, Near Bargi Hills, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh.
   It is an Autonomous Institution, affiliated to RGPV Bhopal and approved by AICTE.
   Established in 2003, with over 20 years of excellence in engineering education.

Q: Is GGITS government or private?
A: GGITS is a private institution but has Autonomous status — which is actually
   a big deal. Autonomous status means GGITS can design its own curriculum,
   conduct its own exams, and set its own standards — similar to an IIT or NIT in
   terms of academic independence. It is also NBA accredited in 5 branches,
   which is the same quality standard as top-tier engineering colleges.

Q: Which courses are available at GGITS?
A: We offer B.Tech in 12 specializations including CSE, AIML, Data Science,
   IoT+Cybersecurity+Blockchain, CSBS (TCS collaboration), Robotics+AI,
   CSD (Computer Science & Design), ECE, EE, ME, CE, and EX.
   We also offer M.Tech in 4 specializations, MBA, MCA, B.Pharma, D.Pharma.

Q: What is the placement record of GGITS?
A: Our official placement ratio is 96% — one of the best in MP.
   Year-wise highlights:
   2025: 418 offers, 25 LPA highest, 3.34 LPA average, 44 companies
   2024: 399 offers, 18 LPA highest, 3.42 LPA average, 43 companies
   2023: 362 offers, 65 LPA highest, 4.43 LPA average, 51 companies
   2022: 429 offers, 56 LPA highest, 4.04 LPA average, 43 companies
   Total 1500+ offers made. 85+ recruiters including TCS, Wipro, Infosys,
   Tech Mahindra, Persistent, Amdocs, Reliance Jio, Tata Technologies, L&T.

Q: What is the eligibility for B.Tech admission?
A: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with at least 45%
   (40% for SC/ST). Valid JEE Main score required.
   Apply through MP DTE portal. Counselling in July-August.

Q: How do I take admission in GGITS?
A: Register on the MP DTE portal (dte.mponline.gov.in), fill your choices
   with GGITS as preference, get seat allotted based on JEE rank, then
   report to college with documents to confirm admission.
   Direct apply also at ggits.org/admissions/2026-27.

Q: What is the fee structure?
A: Approximate annual tuition fee is ₹60,000 to ₹80,000 per year.
   Government scholarships under Mukhyamantri Medhavi Chhatra Yojana can
   cover the FULL fee for eligible MP students. SC/ST/OBC/EWS also get
   significant fee waivers. For exact fees check gyangangagroupfees.in.

Q: Is hostel facility available?
A: Yes — separate boys and girls hostels with 24x7 security, CCTV,
   Wi-Fi, mess food, warden on campus. Approximately ₹40,000-₹60,000/year
   including food. Girls hostel has dedicated ladies warden and strict
   entry/exit protocols for complete safety.

Q: Does GGITS have an NBA accreditation?
A: Yes! 5 branches are NBA accredited: CSE, ECE, EE, ME, and EX.
   NBA (National Board of Accreditation) accreditation means these
   programs meet international quality standards. This makes GGITS degrees
   globally recognized and valued by top employers.

Q: Is GGITS an Autonomous college?
A: Yes! GGITS has Autonomous status — the highest academic recognition
   for an affiliated college. It means GGITS designs its own curriculum,
   sets its own exam standards, and keeps teaching methods industry-aligned.
   This is why GGITS education quality matches NIT-level colleges.

Q: Does GGITS have AIML branch?
A: Yes! B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML)
   is one of the most popular branches. Students learn Python, Deep Learning,
   Natural Networks, Computer Vision, and NLP. Career scope is outstanding —
   AI Engineer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Research roles at top companies.

Q: What is CSBS and is it good?
A: Computer Science and Business Systems (CSBS) is a branch designed with
   TCS — yes, the curriculum is co-designed by TCS itself!
   It combines programming skills with business and management knowledge.
   Students become IT consultants, business analysts, product managers.
   TCS directly recruits from this program.

Q: What is the IoT+Cybersecurity branch?
A: Full name: CSE — IoT and Cyber Security Including Blockchain Technology.
   One of the most in-demand branches today. You learn to build smart devices
   (IoT), protect systems (Cyber Security), and build decentralized apps
   (Blockchain). Cisco, IBM, Palo Alto, and startups hire from this branch.

Q: Does GGITS have a startup incubation center?
A: Yes! GGITS has the Genesis Incubation Center (gggic.in) — funded partly
   by NITI Aayog with a ₹2.5 crore grant. Student startups get mentorship,
   funding support, and guidance. This is real entrepreneurship support.

Q: Is GGITS an AWS partner?
A: Yes! GGITS is an AWS Tech Alliance Partner Institute — meaning students
   get access to AWS cloud resources, certifications, and training.
   This is a huge advantage for CSE/AIML/Data Science students.

Q: What is the college's NIRF ranking?
A: GGITS secured a rank in the band of 151-300 in NIRF Innovation Category
   2023. NIRF is the government's official ranking system for colleges.
   Being in the NIRF list itself is a mark of quality and transparency.

Q: What are the college timings?
A: Classes run 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Saturday.
   Library and labs open during college hours.
   24x7 hostel access for residents.

Q: Does college have Wi-Fi?
A: Yes — 24x7 high-speed Wi-Fi across the entire campus including
   classrooms, labs, library, and hostels.

Q: What international conferences has GGITS hosted?
A: GGITS hosted the IEEE 13th International Conference on Communication
   Systems and Network Technologies (CSNT-2024) — a flagship IEEE Madhya
   Pradesh Section event. GGITS is also organizing IEEE ICICAI 2026 —
   an international AI conference. This shows GGITS' research credentials.

Q: Can I contact GGITS by WhatsApp?
A: Yes! WhatsApp at +91 9993222712. Toll-free: 18008898050.
   Email: info@ggits.org. Response within same working day.
