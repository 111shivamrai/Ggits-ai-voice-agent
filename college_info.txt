ABOUT THE COLLEGE
------------------
Established in 2003, Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology and Sciences (GGITS)
is a premier AICTE-approved, AUTONOMOUS engineering, pharmacy, and management
institution in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, affiliated with Rajiv Gandhi
Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV), Bhopal.

GGITS is recognized as one of the top private engineering colleges in Central India,
known for academic excellence, innovation, industry integration, and strong
placement outcomes. With 5 branches NBA accredited and GGITS being designated
an Autonomous Institution, it offers the highest quality of technical education.
